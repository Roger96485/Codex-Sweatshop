# Codex Sweatshop

Codex's 5-hour quota windows are such a pain. Put your Codex to work around the clock in a sweatshop and squeeze every last drop out of each quota window!

<img src="desktop/icon.png" width="96" height="96" alt="Codex Sweatshop icon">

This is a local Windows app for ChatGPT (Codex Desktop). Create tasks, schedule them into an idle 5-hour quota window, then enjoy a good night's sleep and wake up to the results. You can also adjust when your 5-hour quota windows activate to get the most out of every window.

[Download the Windows release](https://github.com/Roger96485/Codex-Sweatshop/releases/latest)

## Getting started

Unzip the archive from **Releases**, open **Codex Sweatshop.exe**, and you're ready to go!

The app needs the runtime and resource files alongside it. Keep them together.

Sweatshop automatically finds and connects to the ChatGPT app (Codex Desktop) you're already signed in to on your computer. No other dependencies to install.

Keep your computer on, awake, and connected to the internet, with Sweatshop running in the background.

## Squeeze Codex with Sweatshop

1. Select a conversation, enter a prompt, and choose a model to create a task.
2. Drag the task onto the schedule on the left to place it in a quota window. Hold **Alt** to set an exact start time, or expand the task and click **Auto-schedule** to assign it automatically.

## Activate 5-hour quota windows on your terms

As you probably know, if you leave Codex idle after a quota reset, the quota window keeps sliding forward. What a waste!

Codex Sweatshop helps you activate quota windows on time by sending Codex a simple "hello" at the right moment. Don't worry: it uses the cheapest Luna model, with negligible quota consumption.

Each node in the schedule on the left marks the projected end of a quota window. You can:

- Click the unlocked icon beside an available node to schedule a "hello" and activate the window on time.
- Drag a node later to activate the window at a time of your choosing.

## Easter egg: Whip Codex into working harder

Click the whip toggle in the upper-right corner to enable **Whip**. Configure your preferred motivational messages in its settings.

In Codex Desktop, click an empty input box and press **Ctrl+Alt+W** to summon the whip. Swing your mouse quickly to whip Codex and select your favorite motivational message. Press **Esc** to cancel.

When you're done whipping, your chosen message is sent to Codex as a prompt. Codex will work hard under your encouragement.

**Whip**'s assets and inspiration come from **GitFrog1111**'s **OpenWhip** project: [GitFrog1111/OpenWhip](https://github.com/GitFrog1111/OpenWhip).
